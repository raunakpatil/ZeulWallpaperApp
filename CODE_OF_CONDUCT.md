# Code of Conduct

Zeul is built by and for a community. We want it to be a friendly place for everyone, whatever their
experience, background, identity or how they look.

## What we expect

- Be kind and assume good intent.
- Give and take feedback on the work, not the person.
- Help newcomers; everyone started somewhere.
- Respect people's time, privacy and boundaries.
- Credit other people's work, and only share images you have the right to share.

## What's not okay

- Harassment, insults, slurs, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexual or violent content, in discussions or in uploaded wallpapers.
- Sharing someone's private information without their permission.
- Trolling, spam, or repeatedly derailing conversations.

## Where this applies

Issues, pull requests, Discussions, reviews, and wallpapers uploaded to Zeul.

## Reporting

Email **zeulwallpapers@gmail.com**, or use "Report" on a wallpaper in the app. Reports are handled
privately by the maintainers. We may remove content, lock threads, or block accounts (from the repo
or from uploading) when needed.

This code is adapted from the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
