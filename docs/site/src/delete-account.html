Delete your {{APP_NAME}} account

Published by {{PUBLISHERS}}

Option 1: in the app (instant)

  1. Open {{APP_NAME}} and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile → Delete account.
  3. Confirm. Your account is deleted immediately and you're signed out.

Option 2: by email (if you no longer have the app)

Email {{CONTACT_EMAIL}} from the address you signed up with, with the subject "Delete my account". We'll confirm and delete it within 30 days.

What gets deleted

DataWhat happens
Account (email, name, profile photo)Deleted permanently
Favourites, unlocked wallpapers, style preferences and interest scoresDeleted permanently
Past usage events (views, downloads)Unlinked from you (anonymised) immediately, then deleted automatically within 90 days
Reports you submittedUnlinked from your account; the report text is kept while the complaint is handled
SubscriptionNot cancelled automatically. Cancel it in Google Play (Payments & subscriptions) before deleting your account. Google Play and RevenueCat keep purchase records as required for billing, tax and fraud prevention

Deleting your account in the app also removes its favourites, unlocks and style picks from that device. Data saved on a device without an account is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data in Android settings.
