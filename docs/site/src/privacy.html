3. How we use it

  • To run the app: show wallpapers, keep your favourites, count downloads, remember unlocks.
  • To personalise the "For You" feed from the tags of wallpapers you like (only if you're signed in or picked styles).
  • To keep the service safe: rate-limit abuse and review reported content.
  • To show ads to free users, which pay for the free wallpapers.

Legal bases (where GDPR applies): contract for account features, consent for personalised ads, and legitimate interests for statistics, recommendations and security. Under the DPDP Act we process personal data on the basis of your consent, which you can withdraw at any time.

5. Ads and your choices

Free users see ads, including optional "watch an ad to unlock" videos. Google may use your advertising ID to show personalised ads. If you're in the EEA, UK or Switzerland, we ask for your consent before any ad is requested, and you can change it later from Profile → Privacy settings in the app. Anyone can reset or delete their advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.

6. How long we keep data

  • Usage events: automatically deleted after 90 days.
  • Search terms: automatically deleted after 180 days.
  • Daily per-wallpaper totals (which contain no personal data): up to 400 days.
  • Account, favourites, unlocks and preferences: until you delete your account.
  • Reports: as long as needed to handle the complaint.

7. Your rights and deleting your data

You can access, correct or delete your personal data, withdraw consent, and (where GDPR applies) object to processing or request a copy of your data.

  • In the app: Profile → Delete account permanently deletes your account, favourites, unlocks and style preferences, on our servers and on that device. Your past usage events are kept only in anonymised form (unlinked from you) until they expire. Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription: cancel it in Google Play first. Google and RevenueCat keep purchase records as required for billing, tax and fraud prevention.
  • Without the app: follow the steps on our account deletion page.
  • Anything else: email {{CONTACT_EMAIL}}. We reply within 30 days.

If you're unhappy with our answer, you can complain to your data protection authority (in India, the Data Protection Board of India).

8. Security

Data travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Database access is restricted so each user can only read their own private data. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your data and will notify you as the law requires if a breach affects you.

9. Children

{{APP_NAME}} is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has given us personal data, contact us and we'll delete it.

10. Changes

We'll update this page when our practices change, update the date at the top, and tell you in the app about important changes.
