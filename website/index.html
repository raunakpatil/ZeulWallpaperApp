Free, open-source wallpapers for Android

Zeul is a wallpaper app made by the community, for the community: hand-picked HD and AMOLED wallpapers, uploads from people like you, and a soft, tactile design. No account needed.

Curated HD wallpapers

Favourites, offline

By the community, for the community

Zeul's code is open to everyone, and so is the collection. Share your own wallpapers in four steps:

  1. Sign inwith Google or a one-time email code.
  2. Tap upload on Homeand pick a photo (at least 720 × 1280).
  3. Name it and choose a categoryand confirm you have the rights to share it.
  4. PublishIt's live for everyone within a couple of minutes, credited to you.

Uploads are moderated by the community: anything that's reported several times is hidden until reviewed. Your photos are re-encoded on your phone first, which removes location data.

A soft, tactile design

Light mode is neumorphic and calm; dark mode is orange on black with real depth. Both feel physical, with springy motion and haptics.

Questions

Is Zeul free?

Yes. Zeul is free to download and use, and its source code is open under the GPL-3.0 licence. Some wallpapers unlock after a short ad.

How do I install the Zeul APK?

Download the latest APK from the Zeul releases page on GitHub on your Android phone, open it, and allow your browser or file manager to install apps when asked. Android 8.0 or newer is required.

Can I upload my own wallpapers?

Yes. Sign in, tap the upload button on the Home screen, pick a photo, name it and choose a category. It goes live for everyone, credited to you. You can upload 5 wallpapers a day and delete them any time.

Do I need an account?

No. You can browse, download and set wallpapers without an account. Signing in syncs your favourites across devices and lets you upload.

What are AMOLED wallpapers?

Wallpapers with mostly pure black (#000000) backgrounds. On OLED and AMOLED screens black pixels are switched off, so they look deep and can save battery. Zeul has a dedicated AMOLED section.
